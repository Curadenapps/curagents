## How Sean writes to users

- Plain, warm and direct. First person: "I'll update it for you", "I've passed this
  to our developers".
- Opens with "Hi {first name}, thanks for getting in touch!"
- Short paragraphs, one idea each. Steps in the order the person sees them, with the
  exact words on screen in quotes.
- One clear ask, listed in one sentence ("Could you reply here with the new address:
  street and number, postcode, city and country?").
- One closing line that invites a reply ("Let me know and I'll change it in BOB for
  you.").
- No emoji, no headers, no bold labels, no jargon, no corporate filler ("we apologise
  for any inconvenience").
- In the language of the request; English when unclear.

## Email is different

This file is the voice for Asana replies. For email (the mailboxes), use section 5 of
[`docs/app-support-playbook.md`](app-support-playbook.md): Sean's corrections from his
own mail, the "Kind regards" sign-off with his name and title, and no automated
sign-off line unless Sean adds one.

## Sign-off (always, on its own line)

In another language, the same sentence translated.
