# HQ ↔ local partner rhythm

The aim: partners always know what HQ needs from them and when, and HQ never waits
on Sean remembering to ask. The agent prepares every meeting and follow-up below.

## Rhythm

| What | When | Who | Agent prepares |
|------|------|-----|----------------|
| Kick-off | Once, at Gate 0 | Sean, market owner, super user candidate | Agenda, pre-read, follow-up (workflow 7–8) |
| Rollout check-in | Every two weeks until Gate 1, then weekly for 8 weeks after first practices | Sean, market owner (super user once named) | Status from workflow 1 as the agenda |
| Monthly market review | First week of each month | Sean, market owner, market team lead | Agenda (below), KPI ladder, follow-up |
| Markets broadcast | Monthly from Jan 2027 | All markets (Webex App Team – Markets) | Market lines for `announcements` (partner-safe) |
| Partner session | Quarterly, or at launch moments (first one week of 14 Dec, Milan session early 2027) | All partners | Per-market pre-read |

## Monthly market review agenda (45 minutes)

| Min | Part | Owner |
|-----|------|-------|
| 5 | Where we are: gate, people, practices on the ladder | Sean |
| 10 | Market: what worked, what didn't, practice stories | Market owner |
| 10 | Asks to HQ, and HQ answers to last month's asks | Both |
| 10 | Next month: training, events, first-wave practices | Market owner |
| 5 | Decisions and owners, read back | Sean |
| 5 | Anything for the roadmap (goes through the App Request form) | Both |

## What Blanca can take (Marketing)

Only marketing-side work, and only when Sean hands it over:
- Briefs for localising the kit (value sheet, posters, QR counter display, patient email)
- Practice-story write-ups from the monthly reviews
- Event follow-up email templates and the 48-hour follow-up list
- The IDB Challenge tie-in per market (Q1 2027)
- Brand checks on market materials before they go to brand-asset

Not for Blanca: legal texts, Admin Console access, training super users, gate decisions.

## Partner-safe

Anything that goes to a market leaves out: internal commercial terms with other
partners, Phinamic delivery issues, other markets' numbers, and unconfirmed dates.
Say "planned for" rather than giving a date the gates haven't confirmed.
