## Module template

Every module page uses these headings, in this order:

```
Callout: Code · Audience · App · Markets · Format · Minutes · Status · Last checked against build {version/date}

## Why this matters
One paragraph: what the person can do after it, tied to a stage of the activation ladder.

## Before you start
Prerequisite modules, access needed, materials to have open.

## Outline
| Time | Part | What happens | Material |
Timed steps that add up to the module's minutes.

## Try it
One practical exercise on the demo practice or a role-play, with what "good" looks like.

## Check
3–5 questions or one observed task. Answers in a toggle for the trainer.

## Materials
Links: BOB Kit, how-to videos, consent texts, posters, QR displays (localised versions per market).

## Localisation notes
Per market: language(s), the local agreement or consent text to use, wording to avoid,
local examples. Left as [confirm: …] until the market checks it.

## Sources
Notion pages, release notes and Asana tasks the content comes from.
```

## Writing rules

- Plain, short sentences in English; the market translates. Write so a translation
  stays accurate: no idioms, no wordplay.
- Describe the current build only. Anything not built yet: `[confirm: screen in current build]`.
- "Home-care plan" and "recommended products". Never "diagnosis", "treatment", "prescription".
- Any statement about health outcomes (bleeding, gum health, plaque) ticks **Clinical
  wording** and goes to Legal before `Ready`.
- One module, one job. If an outline runs past its minutes, split it.
