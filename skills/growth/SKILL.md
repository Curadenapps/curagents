# Growth

The working method for `agents/growth.md`. Marketing no longer supports BOB (Sean,
25 Nov 2026), so acquisition is run by Sean, the market partners and this skill. The
skill turns "I have no clue how to do this" into a short list of things to publish,
send or decide each week, and a record of what worked.

## Read first

| File | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| `references/funnel.md` | The G1–G4 funnel, how each step is counted, targets worked back from K1, the pulse and review formats |
| `references/channels.md` | Every channel we can use without a marketing team: who acts, effort, cost, how it's measured, the asset it needs |
| `references/messaging.md` | What BOB is in one line, the messages per audience, words to use and avoid, the claims rule, CTAs and links |

Also: `dream.md` §2 (claims, ownership), the Launch KPIs page, the market's page in the
Markets playbook, and `my-writing-style` for anything in Sean's name.

## How to work

1. **Start from the target.** Every plan starts at the market's K1 target and launch
   date (Launch KPIs, market page) and works back through the funnel ratios in
   `funnel.md`. Say the numbers: "to get 8 approved clinics by day 90 we need about 20
   registrations, so about 200 clinics reached."
2. **Pick few channels, do them well.** At most three channels per market at a time,
   chosen from `channels.md` by who is there to act (Sean's hours, the partner's
   sales reps, an event in the window). Partner channels come first: they already
   have the relationships and the consent.
3. **One experiment per idea.** Before drafting anything, write or find its
   experiment row: hypothesis, funnel step, metric and target, owner, end date.
4. **Draft in the calendar.** Each post, email or kit item is a Content calendar row
   with the full text in the page body, Status `Draft`, the market, the languages,
   **Who publishes**, and the experiment it belongs to. Clinical wording → tick it and
   set `Legal check`.
5. **Hand over, don't do.** Ends every run with `for_sean` and `for_partner`: what to
   publish or send, where, by when, with the link.
6. **Close the loop.** The pulse checks which rows were published and what moved;
   the monthly review keeps, changes or stops each experiment and says why.

## Drafting rules

- Sean's LinkedIn posts: his voice (`my-writing-style`), first person, short, one idea
  per post, one link or CTA, no hashtag walls (three at most), no emoji headers.
  Real things: a clinic story the partner shared, a screen from the app, a number
  from BOB Admin (aggregated, no clinic named without its OK).
- Partner material: written for the partner to send in their own name, with
  `{partner name}`, `{rep name}` and `{market language}` placeholders, and a note on
  what to localise.
- Every piece has one CTA. For clinics: register on the form
  (https://curaprox-apps.com/contact-form) or open the web app (https://bobscore.app).
  Questions go to info@bobscore.com.
- No clinical or efficacy claims (see `references/messaging.md`). When in doubt,
  describe what the clinician does with BOB, not what it does to the patient.
- No personal data of clinics or patients in Notion pages, posts or rows. Call lists
  stay in chat or with the partner.

## When data is missing

Say so and use the closest signal: no Webflow analytics → count form submissions;
no BOB Admin on the cloud → use the Monthly market stats rows; a market without a
launch date → plan in relative weeks and mark the date `[confirm]`. Never invent
numbers; mark estimates as estimates.
