# Messaging

## BOB in one line

BOB (Bleeding on Brushing) is a chairside index for dental professionals: go space by
space, record the right interdental brush size and whether the gap bleeds, and the app
turns it into a score, a dental chart and a personal home-care plan emailed to the
patient. Tablet, web (bobscore.app) and, from 14 Dec 2026, phone. Source of truth:
BOB Kit and "What is the BOB App?" in Notion. Re-read them before a campaign; they
change.

## Who we talk to, and what they care about

| Audience | What they care about | Message |
|----------|----------------------|---------|
| Dental hygienists and prophylaxis assistants (the coaches) | Doing a good recall in the time they have; patients who follow through | "A structured way to run the IDB part of the recall, and a plan your patient takes home." |
| Practice owners and dentists | Patient loyalty, recall rate, a modern practice | "Patients see their own progress from visit to visit, so they come back." Describe, don't promise numbers. |
| iTOP-trained clinicians | Applying iTOP in daily work | "BOB puts the iTOP approach into the chair, for the whole team." iTOP isn't required to use BOB. |
| Partners and subsidiaries | Their customers, sell-through of IDBs | "A reason to visit every clinic, and a tool the clinic uses every week." (internal message, not for clinics) |

## Words

- Use: home-care plan, interdental brush (IDB) size, chairside, progress, recall,
  patient record, in a few minutes `[confirm session length before quoting a number]`.
- Avoid: prescription, diagnosis, treatment, cure, prevents, reduces bleeding,
  clinically proven, better than floss, any score band presented as a health verdict.
- Product names: "BOB" for the product, "Curaprox - BOB" for the store listing;
  don't mix "BOB Score" and "BOB App" in one piece.

## The claims rule

No clinical or efficacy claims without Legal's OK (`dream.md` §2): describe what the
clinician does and sees, not what happens to the patient's health. Anything that
crosses the line is ticked **Clinical wording**, goes to `Legal check` and through
`brand-asset`. When unsure, it crosses the line.
