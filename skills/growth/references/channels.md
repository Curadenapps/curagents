# Channels without a marketing team

Pick at most three per market at a time. Partner channels first: they already have
the relationships and the consent. Effort is Sean's or the partner's time per month;
cost is money. Anything with a cost needs Sean's yes before it's planned.

| Channel | Who acts | Effort | Cost | Measured by | What the agent drafts |
|---------|----------|--------|------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Partner's own customers** (email or WhatsApp to the clinics they already sell to) | Partner sales or marketing | 2–4 h | none | G2 from that market in the 2 weeks after the send; partner's open/reply counts | Invite email, short message, follow-up, in the partner's name (`partner kit`) |
| **Partner sales visits** | Partner reps | in their visits | none | Registrations with the rep named in the form notes | 2-minute pitch, leave-behind flyer copy with QR, the 48-hour call script |
| **iTOP courses and alumni** | iTOP trainers, Sean | 1–2 h | none | Registrations from course weeks; question in the form | One slide + demo for the end of a course, alumni email (BOB is for every clinician, taught through iTOP; iTOP isn't required) |
| **Events and congresses** | Partner booth, Sean when there | 1–3 days | booth is the partner's | Lead sheet count → G2 within 14 days | `event prep`: demo script on an anonymous session, QR to register, follow-up day 1/3/7 |
| **Sean's LinkedIn** | Sean | 1 h/week | none | Impressions (G1), profile/website clicks, DMs | 2 posts a week (`content calendar`) |
| **Curaden / Curaprox company pages** | Whoever runs them [confirm owner now marketing is out] | — | none | Impressions | Partner-style post Sean can forward |
| **Website** (curaprox-apps.com BOB pages EN/DE/IT) | `webflow` agent after Sean's go | 1–2 h | none | Visits (G1), form submissions (G2) | `website check`: clear CTA, register in two clicks, FAQ schema, DE/IT parity, AEO answers |
| **App stores** (listed as "Curaprox - BOB") | Phinamic or Sean updates the listing | 1 h | none | Store page views, installs | `store listing check`: subtitle, keywords, screenshots, description per language |
| **Referral by coaches** (a clinic invites a clinic) | Clinics, partner | low | none until a reward exists | Registrations naming a referrer | A "send this to a colleague" message for active clinics; the referral program task in Asana (`1218693312643070`) is the product side |
| **Webinars** (30 min: what BOB is, live demo, Q&A) | Sean or a partner's educator | 3 h incl. prep | none | Sign-ups → G2 within 14 days | Invite, run sheet, follow-up email |
| **Clinic stories** (partners share one a month, per Launch KPIs) | Partner collects, Sean posts | 1 h | none | Feeds the other channels | Interview questions, a short case post (no clinical claims, clinic's written OK) |

## Not without Sean's explicit yes

Paid ads, paid tools, sponsorships, printing, giveaways or incentives, buying any
contact list (never), and any channel that contacts people who didn't opt in.
